## Project Overview
This project focuses on the analysis of methane emissions data for 2022, with particular attention to emission patterns and the contribution of methane to global warming.

The analysis explores methane emission figures, examines data consistency and applies Global Warming Potential (GWP) metrics to understand the climate impact of methane emissions.

## Project Objectives
- Examine methane emission data for 2022.
- Analyse emission patterns and identify variations in reported figures.
- Explore the contribution of methane emissions to climate change.
- Apply Global Warming Potential metrics to assess the warming impact of methane over different time horizons.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Analysis
- Spreadsheet Functions
- Global Warming Potential (GWP) Analysis

## Methodology
The project involved reviewing and extracting relevant methane emissions data, checking data consistency and applying the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6) Global Warming Potential values.

Two assessment periods were considered:
- **GWP100:** 27.9
- **GWP20:** 81.2

## Key Focus
The analysis highlights the importance of methane emissions monitoring and the role of accurate emissions data in understanding climate change and supporting environmental sustainability initiatives.

## Conclusion
This project strengthened my understanding of emissions data analysis and demonstrated how spreadsheet-based analytical techniques can be applied to environmental and energy-related research.
